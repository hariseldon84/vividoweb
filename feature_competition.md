## Priority Website Copy Additions (ordered by impact)

2. **Sentinel Audio™ section** — needs its own feature card like transcript editing has
3. **Captions feature** — one-click captions is a top Riverside/Descript claim, Vivido has it planned
4. **Show notes / AI descriptions** — expand Smart Publish claims
5. **"Free to try" signal** — Descript has a free tier; Vivido should mention trial/early access pricing
6. **AI translation** — neither Vivido's site nor roadmap covers this; high user demand
7. **Remote recording** — Riverside's core claim; Vivido doesn't address it at all
8. **Review / G2 badges** — add when reviews accumulate

## Hero Copy Gaps vs. Competitors

Riverside hero: **"Studio-level quality. No studio required."** + feature list
Descript hero: **"AI-editing for every kind of video"** + "direct your AI co-editor"

Vivido current: **"The studio that learns how you create"** — strongest positioning of the three.
Gap: The intelligence angle isn't backed by enough proof-points in the hero itself.
Fix: Lead with the intelligence hook → immediately list what it does (record, edit, repurpose, publish, learn) → social proof strip below CTA.
